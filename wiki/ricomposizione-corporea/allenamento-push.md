# [cite_start]Scheda Allenamento PUSH + ABS [cite: 53]

[cite_start]Focus su Petto, Spalle, Tricipiti e Addome[cite: 54].

## [cite_start]Regole Tecniche [cite: 55]
* [cite_start]**Focus**: Muscoli di spinta[cite: 56, 57].
* [cite_start]**Recupero**: 2 min per esercizi pesanti (Push-up, Military), 60-90 sec per il resto[cite: 58].
* [cite_start]**Intensità**: Rallenta la discesa (3-4 sec) se i push-up diventano facili[cite: 59].

## [cite_start]Workout Push [cite: 60, 61]
1. [cite_start]**Push-Up**: $3 \times Max$ (Buffer 1-2 rep dal cedimento)[cite: 61].
2. [cite_start]**Military Press**: $3 \times 8-12$ (Rest: 2 min)[cite: 61].
3. [cite_start]**Floor Press Manubri**: $3 \times 10-12$ (Rest: 90 sec)[cite: 61].
4. [cite_start]**Alzate Laterali**: $3 \times 12-15$ (Rest: 60 sec)[cite: 61].
5. [cite_start]**Overhead Extension (Manubrio)**: $3 \times 10-12$ (Rest: 90 sec)[cite: 61].
6. [cite_start]**Triceps Pushdown (Elastici)**: $3 \times 12-15$ (Rest: 60 sec)[cite: 61].

## [cite_start]Workout Addome [cite: 62, 63]
1. [cite_start]**Sit-Up con Sovraccarico**: $3 \times 12-15$ (Rest: 60 sec)[cite: 63].
2. [cite_start]**Russian Twist con Peso**: $3 \times 16-20$ (Rest: 60 sec)[cite: 63].
3. [cite_start]**Crunch agli Elastici**: $3 \times 12-15$ (Rest: 60 sec)[cite: 63].
